## Scope

Salient web app, single screen (src/App.tsx and components). Mode: Operate. Priority confirmed by user: credibility, a serious research instrument whose findings get cited. Must not feel alarmist, activist-poster or playful.

## Audience and task

Compliance, ESG and procurement leads arrive with an ERP supplier export, screen it, triage the salience register, review uncertain matches, and export a cited risk register for a modern slavery statement. Secondary scene: 30-second LinkedIn demo and screenshots.

## Constraints

No third-party requests at runtime (fonts self-hosted from npm packages). Hallmark craft floor is pinned by the user: tokens at :root in OKLCH, two families and two weights at most, untracked sentence-case labels, no em dashes in copy, one shadow at most (none used), no card in card, focus-visible instant, reduced-motion fallbacks, overflow-x clip, capped centred container, tables with horizontal rules only. Do not imitate any real government form identity: no official form numbers, seals or agency marks.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Salient is an entry summary for human rights risk. Every supplier is an entry typed onto a ruled form; every finding is a reviewer's annotation ranked by harm to people. It refuses the KPI-card ESG dashboard and the red-alert activist board.

OWN-WORLD: Cool form stock paper, blue-black ink, and drop-out green for everything printed (box rules, box numbers, labels). Everything the user supplied is typed in Courier Prime; the printed form is Public Sans at 400 and 700. Severity bands are deep oxide, ochre and slate on a left rule. Status is rule form: solid flagged, dashed needs review, hairline no listing, hatched not assessed. Severity and likelihood are a fixed five-box check ramp. Reference codes F-01 and S-01 cross-link everything. No cards, no shadows.

STORY: The lead sees a blank numbered form that shows exactly what will be read, drops a CSV or tries the sample, watches the entries type in, reads the register most salient first, sees what needs review and their leverage, and exports with citations, believing nothing was overclaimed.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A form head strip with the wordmark left and four numbered boxes (supplier list, data editions, processed in this browser, date). A seven-word headline. Box 1 holds the drop zone with the one filled ink button and the sample button. Below, the empty register's ruled columns with dotted fill lines.

FORM: Customs entry summary with drop-out ink, candidate 4 of 7 on the ordered list, seed key 7b167e29. Signature interaction: entries type into the form on screening (opacity and transform, 30 to 50ms stagger, instant under reduced motion).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
